LW-PLA or PLA

The walls are sized for foamed lightweight PLA such as Bambu PLA Aero (about {{MASS_LW}} g of printed parts). Regular PLA prints them fine but weighs about {{MASS_PLA}} g, which needs more power and shortens flights by roughly a third. For PLA, thinner walls (a 0.45 mm single-line wing skin and a 0.8 mm fuselage) bring that down to about 900 g.

Before you print

  • Use a brim on 16_fuse_3_rear: it stands on a thin ring next to the boom-socket column.
  • Turn on thick bridges if your printer bridges poorly. The servo-pocket ceilings and the ring around the camera hole are short bridges.
  • PLA softens around 55 °C. Don't leave the plane in a hot car.
