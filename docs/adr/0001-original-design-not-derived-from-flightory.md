# Original design, not derived from Flightory's Lark files

The plane copies the Flightory Lark's layout and size (pod, pusher behind the wing, single carbon boom, conventional tail, 1290 mm span) but none of its geometry. The Lark is a paid, personal-use design sized for 220 mm beds; buying it and cutting its STLs to fit 180 mm was the alternative. An original design lets every part be shaped for the A1 mini and lets the repo be public. In consequence, no Flightory files, product renders, branding or the "Lark" name go into the repo or the published manual. The plane is called Pusher 1290.
