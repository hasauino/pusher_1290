# Rectangular wing: constant 200 mm chord, no taper

The wing keeps a constant 200 mm chord to the tips. A taper to 140–150 mm at the tip panels was analysed: it improves best L/D by only 1–2 % and slightly *raises* minimum power, which is what endurance (this airframe's job) depends on. It would also make every panel different and bend the straight-spar layout. A 180 mm constant chord gives about 1.6 % more L/D but raises the slowest level-flight speed, which makes hand launches harder. The numbers are in the trade-study table in AERODYNAMICS.md.
