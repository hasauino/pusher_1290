# SD7037 wing airfoil instead of the Lark's E193

The wing uses SD7037 rather than E193, which the Lark uses and this design started with. At the cruise Reynolds number (about 2.3 × 10⁵), NeuralFoil predicts about 14 % less profile drag across the cruise lift range for SD7037, with a similar pitching moment and more maximum lift. That is a 3.8 % gain in best L/D for the whole plane. MH32 was about as good but at 8.7 % thickness leaves too little room around the 10 mm spar sleeve. Details and polars are in AERODYNAMICS.md.
